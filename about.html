About Me

I am a Video Journalist and Digital Creator dedicated to uncovering the soul of India – from the vibrant rhythms of Durga Puja festivals to the timeless beauty of monuments and mandirs. My work spans professional assignments, product‑marketing videos for handlooms, jewelry, and traditional crafts, and personal storytelling that celebrates Indian culture.

Through my YouTube channels I share short‑film documentaries, travel diaries, and creative marketing pieces that blend storytelling with high‑quality cinematography.
