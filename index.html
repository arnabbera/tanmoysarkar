About Tanmoy Sarkar

Over 40 Years of Film Direction & Cinematic Visual Storytelling

40+ Years Film Directing Experience 20+ Years in Mumbai (Ex-Assistant Director with Mahesh Bhatt)

With over 40 years of veteran film directing experience behind the camera, Tanmoy Sarkar is a seasoned visual storyteller whose craft is rooted in deep industry expertise. Having spent more than two decades in Mumbai working as an Assistant Director alongside industry stalwarts like Mahesh Bhatt, Tanmoy brings a wealth of cinematic mastery, precision, and passion to every project he undertakes.

Today, Tanmoy continues to honor his lifelong passion for filmmaking by channeling his rich experience into modern digital media. Specializing in short films, high-impact product marketing, and commercial videography, he collaborates with heritage brands, local artisans, and businesses to showcase exquisite handlooms, handicrafts, and fine jewelry.

Driven by an Unending Curiosity for Indian Culture & Heritage

Uncovering the vibrant rhythm, artistic devotion, and energy of Durga Puja and regional celebrations across Bengal and India.

Documenting the timeless beauty of ancient monuments, sacred mandirs, and authentic local street flavors across Kolkata and Mumbai.

Commercial & Craftsmanship

Crafting high-definition product reels and promotional documentaries for handloom weavers, jewelry houses, and traditional artisans.

Whether you need a commercial brand campaign, artisanal product showcase, or a cultural documentary, Tanmoy combines four decades of cinematic instinct with modern digital vision to tell your story.
