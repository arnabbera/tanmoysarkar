About Tanmoy Sarkar

Professional Filmmaking, Brand Storytelling & Indian Craftsmanship

Two Decades of Filmmaking & Directing Mumbai Assistant Director • Cinevista Communications

With over two decades of professional filmmaking and directing experience behind the camera, Tanmoy Sarkar is a seasoned visual storyteller dedicated to highlighting the richness of Indian craftsmanship. Having spent key years in Mumbai working as an Assistant Director with prominent Bollywood stalwarts and leading production houses like Cinevista Communications, Tanmoy brings cinematic mastery, precision, and narrative depth to every project he undertakes.

Today, Tanmoy channels his extensive industry background into high-impact digital media and strategic brand collaborations. Specializing in commercial videography, short-form storytelling, and visual product marketing, he partners with heritage brands, local artisans, and textile manufacturers to showcase exquisite handlooms, regional handicrafts, and fine traditional art.

Capturing the Heart of Indian Craftsmanship & Culture

From the vibrant rhythms of local festivals and historic landmarks to the intricate details of traditional weaving, Tanmoy crafts visual media that connects brands directly with engaged audiences.
